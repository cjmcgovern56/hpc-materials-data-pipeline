The objective of this study is to quantify how the introduction of the larger Silicon atom into a carbon-based lattice modifies its structural dimensions and total cohesive energy.

* **Structural Hypothesis:** The substitution of Silicon ($r_{Si} = 1.11$ Å) into the Carbon lattice ($r_C = 0.77$ Å) will cause a uniform, predictable volumetric expansion of the equilibrium lattice constant relative to pure diamond.
* **Energetic Hypothesis:** The total energy per atom for 3C-SiC will be less negative (higher in energy) than diamond. This indicates a lower thermodynamic stability despite its immense industrial advantages in high-temperature, high-power semiconductor environments.

---

## 🛠️ Computational Methodology & Workflow

### Step 1: Convergence Testing
To establish a high-precision baseline while optimizing high-performance computing resources, a series of self-consistent field (SCF) calculations were tracked to target total energy stability within a strict threshold of $\Delta E < 1 \text{ mRy/atom}$.

* **Planewave Kinetic Energy Cutoff (`ecutwfc`):** With K-points fixed at $6 \times 6 \times 6$, the planewave cutoff was systematically stepped from 40 Ry to 100 Ry. The energy shift between 80 Ry and 100 Ry stabilized at a negligible $0.07 \text{ mRy}$ for 3C-SiC and $0.12 \text{ mRy}$ for Diamond, making 80 Ry the optimal converged parameter.
* **K-Point Grid Density:** Using the converged 80 Ry cutoff, the Monkhorst-Pack K-point grid density was varied across $4 \times 4 \times 4$, $6 \times 6 \times 6$, and $8 \times 8 \times 8$ configurations.

### Step 2: Variable-Cell Relaxation (`vc-relax`)
Using the pre-converged benchmarks, full geometry and volume optimizations were performed using:
* **Exchange-Correlation Functional:** Perdew-Burke-Ernzerhof (PBE) flavor of the Generalized Gradient Approximation (GGA).
* **Pseudopotentials:** Norm-Conserving Scalar Relativistic (ONCVPSP library).
* **Electronic Smearing:** Gaussian smearing ($0.01 \text{ Ry}$) with spin polarization disabled (`nspin=1`).
* **Optimized K-Grids:** A $6 \times 6 \times 6$ grid was deployed for 3C-SiC to handle the binary polar-covalent environment. For the highly symmetric, homonuclear elemental Diamond lattice, a lighter $4 \times 4 \times 4$ grid was verified to provide equivalent numerical precision with maximum computational efficiency.

## 💡 Summary of Conclusions

* **Structural Hypothesis Confirmed:** Substituting Silicon into the lattice results in a clear +0.56% lattice expansion to a relaxed parameter of 8.28 Bohr (4.379 Å). This structural shift is a direct mechanical necessity driven by the larger atomic volume of Silicon.
* **Experimental Validation:** The calculated 3C-SiC lattice parameter matches experimental literature standards exceptionally well (4.359 Å from Harris, 1995). The slight 0.4% overestimation represents a textbook, well-documented behavior of the PBE-GGA functional framework.
* **Energetic Hypothesis Confirmed:** The energy per atom for 3C-SiC ($-10.27 \text{ Ry}$) is higher (less negative) than Diamond ($-12.06 \text{ Ry}$). This confirms that heteronuclear polar-covalent Si-C bonds occupy a shallower potential energy well than the exceptionally dense, pure covalent C-C bonds found in Diamond, reflecting its lower thermodynamic cohesive strength.
